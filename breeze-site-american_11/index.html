Frequently Asked

Common Questions, Answered

Why are there so many pests in Northwest Florida?
The Gulf Coast's warm, humid climate is the perfect environment for ants, roaches, spiders, rodents and more to thrive year-round. That's why Pensacola-area homes do best with routine, scheduled home protection instead of one-time treatments.
How do I get year-round protection from pests?
Our General Pest Defense plan treats your home on a bi-monthly schedule, targeting the pests that pressure Gulf Coast homes in every season — backed by an unlimited reservice guarantee. If anything shows up between visits, your technician comes back at no charge.
Is professional pest control really more effective than DIY?
Yes. Store-bought products can offer short-term relief, but effective home protection requires the right products, applied correctly, on a recurring schedule. A licensed local technician takes the guesswork — and the hassle — out of it.
Are your treatments safe for children and pets?
Our treatments are pet- and family-conscious by design. Your technician will walk you through any simple precautions at each visit, and we honor pet protocols and scheduling preferences for every home.
How do I set up my first appointment?
Call (850) 400-8445 or request a free quote online. A member of our local team will reach out during business hours to answer questions and get you on the schedule — most first visits happen within 48 hours.
Do you offer termite inspections and treatments?
Yes — termites are one of our specialties. We perform official WDO (Wood-Destroying Organism) inspections with FDACS-compliant reports for home sales, refinancing, or peace of mind, plus subterranean and drywood termite treatments, preventative retreatments, and renewable termite bonds for ongoing protection. Call or request a quote to schedule an inspection.
Do you handle rodents and wildlife too?
Yes. Beyond General Pest Defense, we offer full rodent exclusion and removal, plus humane wildlife removal for raccoons, squirrels, opossums, bats and more. Every specialty job starts with a free assessment and a custom quote.
